RESEARCH PROFILE

Health Informatics PhD candidate with a PhD in econometrics, working at the intersection of statistics, artificial intelligence, and health. Research focuses on causal inference, target-trial emulation, heterogeneous treatment effects, and AI-assisted clinical research using Medicare claims and electronic health records.

TECHNICAL EXPERTISE

Methods: causal inference; target-trial emulation; marginal structural models; heterogeneous treatment effects; causal forests; GenericML; generative AI and multi-agent systems.

Software and data: R, SQL, Python, Stata; Medicare claims and electronic health records.

WORK AND RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

Graduate Research Assistant | University of Missouri | 2023–present

Analyze Medicare claims and electronic health records using SQL and R; implement causal analyses and develop AI-assisted research workflows.

Emulate the ONTARGET antihypertensive trial in US Medicare beneficiaries and investigate heterogeneous treatment effects across randomized and observational data.

Develop agentic approaches for trial emulation, benchmarking, and calibration through TrialCalibre, and LLM-assisted clinical code-set construction through TrialCode Agent.

Study negative-control discovery as a stress test for causal reasoning in LLMs and robustness under restricted interventions in partial causal world models.

Integrate the CMS COST table by transforming raw claims data into standardized datasets.

Develop AI agents for Alzheimer’s disease.
